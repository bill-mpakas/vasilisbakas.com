## **Overview**

Cleanall is a cleaning company that offers a wide range of services, from residential cleaning to commercial and industrial cleaning. The company's website is designed to showcase its services and help customers easily book appointments online. The website is clean, modern, and user-friendly, with a focus on simplicity and ease of use.
